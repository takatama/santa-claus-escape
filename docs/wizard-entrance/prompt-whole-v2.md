# 全身一体版の生成プロンプト

組み込み `image_gen`、透過背景。編集対象は原画 `game/assets/witch/wizard-feet-v2.png`。旧分割素材は入力に使用していません。出力は `game/assets/witch/wizard-whole-v2.png`、配信は同名 `.webp`。元の原画は保持しています。

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the ORIGINAL attached wizard-feet-v2 illustration into a simple game puppet atlas. Preserve this exact young brown-haired girl, same large head, short natural neck mostly HIDDEN by the high purple cape collar, original hat, purple gold-star cape, white dress, boots, staff and paper illustration texture. NOT the previous disassembled puppet. CRITICAL: hat, hair, face, neck, collar, entire body, legs, boots and staff must remain ONE CONTINUOUS anatomically natural drawing in each pose. No long exposed neck. No separated heads, hats, skirts, feet or shoulders. Transparent background. 2x2 atlas of equal square cells with generous empty transparent gutters; each object wholly inside its cell. Top-left: original full-body girl with mouth gently CLOSED, kind eyes, remove ONLY viewer-left extended bare hand at the WRIST; keep entire sleeve and shoulder seamlessly attached to torso. The hand's empty location must be transparent, not an extra hand underneath. Sleeve cuff opening remains and should cover wrist joint. Top-right: the EXACT SAME complete full-body figure, same location, scale, silhouette, clothes and pose, but a sweet gentle happy SMILE. Again viewer-left bare hand absent at wrist. Keep head and body joined. Bottom-left: ONLY that removed bare open hand and a short wrist overlap tab, palm facing viewer, original illustration fingers and skin; no detached arm, sleeve, head, or body. Bottom-right: completely empty transparent. Match top-left and top-right body registration exactly, feet at same baseline. Do not crop hat tip, boots or staff. No text, grid lines or backgrounds. The purpose is that ONLY the bare hand rotates at wrist while the rest of the body remains a single image; a smile change swaps the whole continuous figure.
```

生成結果は1285×1224。左上と右上に、手だけを外した全身の口閉じ／笑顔。左下に手。帽子・顔・首・襟・体・裾・足・杖はそれぞれ全身画像の一部で、実行時に分割・貼り合わせをしません。独立して動くのは手首から先だけです。
